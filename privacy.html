Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

DNC Publisher ("the Application") is a private, single-user tool developed by Vinicius Valim ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Application handles data obtained through the use of third-party APIs.

2. Data We Collect

The Application only processes data that is strictly necessary for its core function — publishing videos to the Developer's own social media accounts. Specifically:

  • OAuth tokens: Access tokens obtained through the platform authorization flow. Stored locally on the Developer's device only. Never transmitted to any third-party server.
  • User identity (open_id, display_name): Retrieved solely to confirm the correct account is authenticated. Not stored beyond the current session.
  • Video files: Uploaded directly from the Developer's device to the platform API. Not stored, copied, or processed by the Application beyond the upload operation.

3. API Scopes and Data Usage

The Application requests the following scopes from the platform API:

Scope Why it is requested How data is used
user.info.basic Verify that the authenticated account belongs to the Developer Display name and open_id are printed to the terminal for confirmation only. Not stored or transmitted.
video.upload Upload pre-edited video files to the platform Video files are sent directly from the Developer's local machine to the platform's upload endpoint. No copy is retained by the Application.
video.publish Publish uploaded videos as posts or drafts on the Developer's account Used only to finalize the upload and set publish status. No content data is retained after the API call completes.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
  • We do not share any data with advertisers or third parties.
  • We do not store video content, captions, or metadata on any server.
  • We do not have access to any other user's account or data.

5. Data Retention

OAuth access tokens are stored in a local file (.tiktok_token.json) on the Developer's own machine. This file can be deleted at any time. Tokens expire automatically as defined by the platform's token lifetime policy.

6. Third-Party Platforms

The Application interfaces with third-party platforms via their official APIs. Those platforms have their own privacy policies that govern how they handle data. The Developer is not responsible for third-party data practices.

7. Security

All API communications are performed over HTTPS. Access tokens are stored locally and are never transmitted to any server controlled by the Developer other than the official platform API endpoints.

8. Children's Privacy

This Application is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
